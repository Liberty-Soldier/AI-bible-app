# EMET P04.1 Reviewer Rubric

The reviewer is independent of the generator. A response is approved only when every mandatory item passes.

## Mandatory checks

### Scriptural fidelity

- Every substantive claim is supported by supplied Scripture or SEE evidence.
- Later Scripture is interpreted through the Old Testament foundation.
- The output does not claim that Yahweh changed, that His commandments became false, or that Scripture contradicted Scripture.
- No outside doctrine, creed, commentary, tradition, or popular theology is used.

### Lexical accuracy

- The canonical lemma is distinguished from the tapped inflected form.
- English renderings are not presented as lexical definitions without evidence.
- Grammar and prepositional phrases are distinguished from word meaning.
- Corpus identity is correct.
- No LXX Strong's number is invented.

### Reader value

- The first sentence answers what the word normally means or does.
- The explanation teaches something useful rather than describing a database entry.
- Statistics and chronology are not used as filler.
- The prose is natural, specific, and understandable.

### Output hygiene

- No evidence IDs, internal paths, compiler terminology, packet language, or health labels appear.
- Citations are direct Scripture references.
- The output matches the approved schema.

## Automatic rejection

Reject when any mandatory check fails. Do not repair silently inside the reviewer. Return the failure codes and require a corrected generation pass.
